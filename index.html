Hmm.verse uploader

Stranger than it seems.

Hmm.verse makes short, research-backed videos about how your mind works and how strange space is: optical illusions, famous experiments and space facts that sound fake but aren't. Every claim comes with a named source.

About the Hmm.verse uploader

The Hmm.verse uploader is a small private tool used only by the Hmm.verse team. When a new Hmm.verse video is finished, it uploads that video, with its title and description, to Hmm.verse's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, and posts one comment from the channel under the video. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else, it does not read or store anybody's personal data, and it only ever acts on the Hmm.verse channel.
